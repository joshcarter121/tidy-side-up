Sunnyside Lane

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy is for Sunnyside Lane (“the game”), made by Joshua Carter (“I”, “me”). It covers the game and this website.

The short version

  • The game collects no personal information from anyone, children included.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your progress is saved on your own device. It is never sent to me.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

What the game does not do

  • It has no sign-up or login, and does not ask for your name, email, age or location.
  • It shows no ads and includes no advertising or analytics tools.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It has no chat, no friends list and no way to talk to other players.
  • It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or location, and it cannot see your Photos.
  • It does not send your game to any server of mine. I do not run any servers for the game.

What stays on your device

To remember where you left off, the game saves a small file on your device. It holds your progress (jobs done, stars, Sparkles, unlocked places and tools), your settings, and the name and van logo you choose for your pool company.

If you use the photo mode or save a results card, the picture is saved in the game’s own storage on your device. It does not go to your Photos library and is not shared.

None of this leaves your device through the game. Deleting the game deletes it. Your phone’s own backups (iCloud or a computer) may include it, depending on your Apple settings, which is between you and Apple.

The company name is a free-text box, so please don’t type anything personal into it.

Purchases

The game is free to download, with optional one-time purchases (the full game, level passes and decoration packs). They are bought through Apple’s App Store with your Apple ID. Apple takes the payment. I never see your card, your name or your Apple ID.

The game asks Apple which purchases you own, so it can unlock them again after a reinstall or on a new phone (“Restore Purchases” in Settings). Apple also gives developers overall sales figures, such as how many were sold in which countries. Those figures do not identify you.

Sparkles, the in-game currency, are earned by playing and are never sold for real money.

Game Center

Game Center is not in the game yet. I may add Apple’s Game Center leaderboards and achievements in a future update. If I do, it will be optional and run by Apple under your Game Center settings and Apple’s privacy policy. Your scores and achievements would go to Apple, and other players might see your Game Center nickname, as your settings allow. I would not receive your name, Apple ID or friends list. This page will be updated before that happens.

Children

The game is made to be enjoyed by people of all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (US COPPA) or under the age set by the law where you live. It has no ads, no chat and no social features.

The only places the game sends you outside itself are the privacy policy and terms pages on this website, from the Settings screen, which open in your browser. Parents can use Apple’s Ask to Buy and Screen Time to control purchases on a child’s device.

Other companies and code

The only outside service the game uses is Apple’s (the App Store and its in-app purchase system, and Game Center if it is added). The game is built with the open-source Godot Engine and an open-source plugin for Apple’s purchase system. Both run on your device and send nothing to anyone in this game. There are no advertising, analytics or social media tools in the game.

This website

These pages are plain text and images. They use no cookies, no scripts and no analytics. They are hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep ordinary server logs of visits under its own privacy statement. I do not see those logs.

Your choices and rights

Because I hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for me to look up, correct, export or delete. To remove everything the game saved, delete the game from your device. I do not sell or share personal information. If you have a question or a request under a privacy law that applies to you, write to me and I will answer.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects what is collected, I will say so in the game’s update notes too.
